External Study Resources

Explore a wide collection of trusted digital libraries, academic repositories, and open-access book platforms. This page brings together resources for study, research, and self-learning, including our college library and well-known global archives. Access textbooks, research papers, and eBooks all in one place.

Visit SDC Digital Library 📚

1. Official Academic & Research Repositories

Trusted portals supported by universities and government bodies. Great for authentic learning, theses, and structured courses.

E-GyanKosh

Developed by IGNOU, E-GyanKosh is a comprehensive repository of self-learning materials, course blocks, study guides, and academic resources. It is particularly valuable for distance-learning students and learners pursuing structured undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

e-PG Pathshala

An initiative of INFLIBNET, e-PG Pathshala provides expertly developed postgraduate-level e-content across a wide range of disciplines. Its structured modules, learning materials, and multimedia resources make it a useful reference for advanced study, examination preparation, and academic research.

Shodhganga

Shodhganga is a national repository of theses and dissertations contributed by Indian universities and research institutions. It offers researchers access to original academic work that can support literature reviews, topic exploration, research design, and the development of dissertations and scholarly papers.

National Digital Library of India

The National Digital Library of India brings together a vast range of educational and scholarly resources from numerous institutions and repositories. Its extensive collection of books, articles, manuscripts, lectures, videos, and other learning materials makes it a valuable starting point for broad academic research and self-directed learning.

2. Open Access Books & Textbooks

Completely free and legal collections of books, textbooks, and classics for study and self-learning.

Directory of Open Access Books

DOAB provides access to a growing collection of scholarly books published under open-access models, with an emphasis on academic quality and peer-reviewed research. It is especially useful for university students, researchers, and academics seeking authoritative books that can be accessed without subscription barriers.

Open Textbook Library

The Open Textbook Library offers a diverse collection of openly licensed textbooks covering disciplines ranging from mathematics and science to business and the humanities. These resources are particularly useful for university coursework, independent study, and educators seeking accessible alternatives to conventional commercial textbooks.

Project Gutenberg

Project Gutenberg is a long-established digital library dedicated primarily to freely accessible public-domain literature. Its extensive collection is well suited to readers and students looking for classic novels, poetry, philosophy, history, and other influential works from earlier periods.

3. Community Digital Libraries

Large collections shared by global communities. While some are unofficial, they help learners access knowledge that may otherwise be hard to find.

Internet Archive

Internet Archive is a nonprofit digital library dedicated to preserving and providing access to books, websites, films, music, software, and other cultural materials. Its collections are particularly valuable for historical research, archival studies, older publications, rare materials, and resources that may no longer be readily available elsewhere.

Library Genesis

Library Genesis is an unofficial digital repository associated with a broad range of books, textbooks, scientific papers, and technical publications. It is known for helping users locate hard-to-find academic material, although the copyright status and legal availability of individual resources can vary by jurisdiction.

Anna’s Archive

Anna’s Archive is an unofficial search platform that aggregates metadata and links relating to books, academic papers, and other digital publications from multiple sources. It can be useful for discovering obscure or difficult-to-locate titles, while users should independently verify the copyright and legal status of any material they access.

Z-Library

Z-Library is an unofficial digital-book platform associated with a large collection of eBooks and academic publications across numerous subjects. It is commonly used to search for textbooks, scholarly books, and general reading material, but the legal status of specific resources should always be checked before accessing or downloading them.

PDF Drive

PDF Drive is a book-search platform historically known for indexing a wide variety of PDF-format reading material across education, technology, literature, business, and other fields. It can serve as a discovery tool for locating books and study resources, with users advised to confirm that individual materials are legally available before downloading them.
